What residents can do

One login gives you access to every service on the Resident Portal.

Report maintenance faults

Choose the fault type, pin the location and attach a photo. You get a reference number straight away.

Submit and track complaints

See the status, the department handling it and every update from municipal officials.

Book public facilities

Check availability for community halls and recreation facilities. Double bookings are blocked automatically.

Pay bills online

View utility bills, pay through a secure gateway and keep your payment receipts.

Apply for a vendor licence

Street vendors can register and apply for a business licence without visiting an office.

Get notified

Receive SMS, email or in-app alerts when a bill, booking or complaint changes.

What municipal staff can do

The Administration Portal is a single dashboard for managing service delivery.

  • Manage resident accounts and profiles
  • Generate and schedule utility bills
  • Verify online payments
  • Assign and track maintenance requests
  • Resolve complaints through set workflows
  • Approve street vendor applications
  • Manage facility bookings and availability
  • Produce financial and performance reports
  • Control access by staff role
  • Send notifications to residents

How it is built

A layered Jakarta EE application, designed to grow from 5,000 to 50,000+ residents.

PresentationHTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript. Resident and Admin portals that work on desktop, tablet and mobile.
APIREST endpoints with JSON, versioning, rate limiting and HTTPS with TLS 1.3.
Business logicResident, billing, payment, fault, complaint, vendor, notification and facility services.
Data accessJPA and Hibernate with connection pooling and Redis caching.
DatabaseMySQL 8.0+ with a primary and replica setup, encrypted backups and replication.
